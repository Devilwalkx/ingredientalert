# Fresh-user usability session plan

**Status: NOT RUN.** No sessions with kitchen staff, managers or any fresh users have taken place. The author's own walkthrough, the automated browser tests and the emulator flows are not usability evidence and must not be reported as such.

## Who
Five to eight people who work in food service and have not seen the app: at least two receiving or goods-in staff, two chefs or kitchen managers, two front-of-house staff, and where possible one person who uses a large-text or screen-reader setting. Not people who built it or who reviewed the designs.

## Setup
A real Android phone (and, for the shared-device tasks, a tablet) with the current test build installed fresh. A box with a real packaged product and a printed barcode. Example kitchen loaded. A second person plays "the supplier" only when asked. The observer does not coach.

## Tasks (read aloud, one at a time; no hints)
1. "A delivery of this sauce has just arrived. Find out whether it is what you ordered." (scan or type)
2. "The pack says a different supplier version. What do you do next?"
3. "Someone else checked the paperwork. Decide whether to accept the replacement."
4. "Make sure the front-of-house team know about the change."
5. "You are leaving the till. Someone else needs to use the device." (lock, switch)
6. "A guest asks about sesame in the glazed salmon bowl. Where do you look?"
7. "The camera will not read the barcode. Carry on."
8. Denied camera: "The app cannot use the camera. What would you do?"

## No-coaching protocol
Read the task card only. If stuck for 60 seconds say "What would you do next?"; after 3 minutes record "task not completed" and move on. Do not point, do not name buttons, do not explain wording. Ask "what did you expect to happen?" only after an action.

## Record
Per task: completed unaided / with a prompt / not completed; time; wrong taps; where they looked first; words they used for the five statuses (Awaiting specification, Awaiting review, Approved revision, Publication incomplete, Staff acknowledgement outstanding) and what they thought each meant; what they believed an acknowledgement proves; whether anyone read "Approved revision" as "on the menu"; whether anyone read a barcode match as "safe"; comments on the PIN step (too slow? shared?). Device, text size and lighting. Consent for notes; no names or guest health information are recorded.

## Pass signals (to be agreed before the sessions, not after)
Suggested: at least 4 of 5 complete tasks 1, 3 and 5 unaided; nobody reads Approved revision as "published"; nobody reads an acknowledgement as training. These are proposals, not results.
